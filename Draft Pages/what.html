THE INFRASTRUCTURE

A Zero-Legacy Model

01

Zero-Legacy Value Model

Built for value alignment, not value extraction. Divergent where legacy opcos are convergent. Recombinant where they are familiar. Bespoke where they upskill and hire. Optimised for pure signal where they are optimised for business as usual.

02

RAG-Based AI Synthesis Layer

The institutional brain that provokes questions, challenges assumptions, synthesises artefacts, and manages logistics. Institutional memory from day one — knowledge that compounds instead of departing with every strategist.

03

Service Velocity Architecture

Two deployment tiers: Tier 1 (Days → Weeks) for intensive, high-velocity strategic challenges — POVs, workshops, pitch war rooms, go-to-market. Tier 2 (Weeks → Months) for deeper integration and embedded transformation.

04

Zero Fixed-Cost Architecture

Zero real estate. Zero idle staff. Zero billable hour logic. Every structural constraint of the legacy agency model has been dissolved. What remains is pure capability, activated on demand.

A ZERO-LEGACY
MODEL

Most attempts to fix agencies focus on surface-level change: flatter hierarchies, better culture, flexible work, AI tools bolted on top. These reforms fail because they don't address the underlying structural problem — the agency model itself is a legacy construct, optimised for a world that no longer exists.

ANTIGEN is not a reform. It is a reconstruction from first principles.

Divergent, not convergent. Built to challenge, not confirm.
Recombinant, not familiar. Bespoke assembly for every problem.
Faster: AI-augmented, with no management layer diluting or delaying ideas.
Better incentivised: talent owns the upside; they are motivated to solve, not to sustain.
More intelligent: knowledge compounds via the Synthesis Layer rather than leaving with every departing strategist.
