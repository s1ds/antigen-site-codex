1
🎯

Trigger / Need Recognition

Transformation pressure, compliance load, and portfolio sprawl create the first opening for PPM.

What happens

Transformation complexity rises. Project tracking fragments. Leaders feel the pain of weak visibility across change portfolios.

Key stakeholders

  • CIO / CTO
  • Transformation Office
  • PMO / EPMO
  • Business Heads

Buyer questions

Why now? What pain is severe enough to solve?

India BFSI nuance

Urgency is created by modernization, compliance pressure, and growth, but budget often needs a sharper governance story.

2
🤝

Internal Mobilisation & Problem Framing

The institution defines the problem and decides whether this is a planning, resource, or governance issue.

What happens

The team maps current planning and governance processes and clarifies the capabilities that are missing.

Key stakeholders

  • PMO / EPMO
  • Strategy
  • Transformation Leads
  • Enterprise Architecture
  • Business Unit Leaders

Buyer questions

Is this a project problem or an operating-model problem?

India BFSI nuance

There are usually multiple change agendas running at once across retail, corporate, operations, and technology.

3
📄

Business Case & Requirements

Value, integration, reporting, security, and audit needs become concrete evaluation criteria.

What happens

The sponsor team builds the ROI case, defines use cases, and captures must-have requirements.

Key stakeholders

  • Sponsor
  • PMO / EPMO
  • Finance
  • CIO team
  • Enterprise Architecture

Buyer questions

How will this improve prioritisation, governance, speed, and capital allocation?

India BFSI nuance

The case usually needs measurable control and productivity gains, not just software efficiency.

4
🔎

Market Scan & Longlist

Vendors are compared against scale, governance depth, and enterprise fit.

What happens

The team scans vendors, analyst views, peer references, and known enterprise platforms.

Key stakeholders

  • PMO
  • CIO office
  • Procurement
  • Architecture

Buyer questions

Which vendors fit our scale, governance model, and support expectations?

India BFSI nuance

Enterprise credibility and implementation maturity matter as much as feature breadth.

5
🧪

Evaluation, Demos & PoC

Fit-gap, sandbox testing, security checks, and reference calls start to narrow the field.

What happens

The buyer runs workshops, fit-gap reviews, scoring, and reference validation.

Key stakeholders

  • Evaluation team
  • Power users
  • InfoSec
  • Implementation lead

Buyer questions

Can it integrate with our stack? Can users adopt it quickly?

India BFSI nuance

Integration, onboarding effort, and process fit often decide whether enthusiasm survives the demo.

6
🛡️

Risk, Compliance & Procurement Diligence

Security, privacy, legal, commercials, and third-party risk gates become decisive.

What happens

The deal goes through risk checks, privacy review, legal scrutiny, SLA review, and procurement negotiation.

Key stakeholders

  • InfoSec
  • Risk
  • Legal
  • Procurement
  • Vendor management

Buyer questions

Is the vendor safe, compliant, and contractable on our terms?

India BFSI nuance

Cloud posture, data residency, and auditability become high-stakes objections here.

7
✅

Approval & Selection

The final recommendation needs executive confidence, not just technical fit.

What happens

The team presents the recommendation and asks for budget release and selection approval.

Key stakeholders

  • Executive sponsor
  • Steering committee
  • Finance
  • Procurement

Buyer questions

Does this align with the transformation roadmap, budget cycle, and operating priorities?

India BFSI nuance

Consensus is rarely enough. A credible business case and risk story are both required.

8
🚀

Deployment & Change Adoption

Implementation, training, migration, and operating model change begin to prove the decision.

What happens

The team designs rollout, configures the platform, migrates data, and trains users.

Key stakeholders

  • Implementation partner
  • Platform owner
  • PMO
  • Change lead

Buyer questions

Who will own the platform, and how fast will adoption happen?

India BFSI nuance

Successful rollouts usually start with a small, high-credibility use case and then expand.

9
📈

Usage, Value Realisation & Expansion

Adoption, reporting quality, and portfolio governance determine whether the platform scales.

What happens

The institution measures adoption, business value, and opportunities to extend the platform to more teams.

Key stakeholders

  • PMO / EPMO
  • Platform owner
  • Business leaders
  • Transformation office

Buyer questions

Are we getting value? What should we expand next?

India BFSI nuance

Renewal and expansion depend on proof that the platform improved control, coordination, and decision speed.
