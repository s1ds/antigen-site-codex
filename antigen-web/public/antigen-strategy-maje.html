The Safe (Wrong) Assumption

"Customers are showrooming us to buy cheaper alternatives online."

The Provocative Reality

Customers want the immediate gratification of the purchase, but your checkout process forces them to sober up. They walk out because the physical act of paying is too slow to sustain the high of the mirror.

To convert high-fashion browsers, we must make the transaction as invisible as the transformation in the mirror.

Phase 03

Provocation

Kill the Museum

Outlaw "Just Looking"

Accessible luxury has a polite, polite disease: we treat our stores like galleries and our customers like art critics. Maje's high foot traffic and low conversion isn't a traffic problem — it's a hospitality trap. We have made browsing too comfortable and buying too secondary.

The Sacred Rule

"Never rush the customer. Make the store a sanctuary of slow discovery."

The Provocation

Sanctuaries are for dying. Maje is a point of transaction. We will weaponise friction at the exit and eliminate it at the point of desire.

Old Paradigm
Browse → Try On → Think About It → Exit
No friction to leave empty-handed
→
The Provocative Shift
Try On → Instant Scan → Walk Out Wearing (Paid)
High social/digital friction to abandon
I
Abolish the Cash Wrap

The traditional cash register is a monument to hesitation. Every mirror in the fitting room is a terminal. Every stylist is a walking, tap-to-pay gateway. If a customer has to walk to the front of the store to pay, we have failed.

II
The Zero-Click Fitting Room

RFID-enabled smart mirrors register the fit the moment a customer hangs an item. A personalised, expiring "In-Store Acquisition Offer" appears. Walking out without tapping "Acquire" triggers a polite scarcity countdown.

III
The Walk-Out Protocol

Put the item on. Tap your phone against the fitting room door sensor. Walk out of the store wearing it. No bagging. No receipt printing. No transition from "I look amazing" to "I am swiping my credit card."

Phase 04

Territory

The Urgency of Immediate Identity

The Grief of the Empty-Handed Exit

To walk into a Maje store is to enter a temporary contract with your most aspirational self. To walk out empty-handed is to breach that contract. We must claim a fierce, defensive emotional territory: The Urgency of Immediate Identity.

Old Emotional Space

"I'll think about it."
Rationalisation, delay, eventual cart abandonment.

→
New Defensive Territory

"If I leave this behind, I am actively choosing a lesser version of myself today."

01
The Fitting Room Is the Point of Sale

Eliminate the physical cash wrap entirely. The second a client feels the emotional high, the transaction is completed via frictionless mobile tap-to-pay inside the fitting room. She wears it out.

02
Rebrand "Hesitation" as "Loss"

Implement the Hold-and-Hazard protocol. If a customer hesitates, she is informed — with genuine regret — that leaving the item means forfeiting it to the next client in line. We do not hold for the hesitant; we reward the decisive.

03
The "Unbought" Tax

Frictionless mobile checkout inside the fitting room unlocks an immediate, exclusive styling benefit. If you go to the traditional queue, you pay the "tax" of wasted time and lose the instant tailoring perk.

"Wear it out, or leave it behind. There is no middle ground."

Safe Ideas · Killed
  • "Omnichannel Integration" — just a slow way of saying "buy it on your phone at home." BYPASS forces the transaction to happen now, in the mirror.
  • "Clienteling Apps" — retail staff hate them and rarely use them effectively. BYPASS shifts agency to the consumer's own device.
  • Standard Mobile POS (Line Busting) — associates chasing customers with iPads is invasive. High-fashion buyers want autonomy, not a shadow.
Phase 06

Execution

Fit-and-Run · The Earned-First Architecture

Burn the Cash Wrap

We are launching an Earned-First execution that turns the transaction into a cultural flex: The Maje "Fit-and-Run." We mimic the thrilling ease of shoplifting — completely legally. Try on, pay via a single tap in the fitting room, walk straight out the front door.

1
The Spark
The Provocation Launch

A targeted, unannounced stunt in flagship locations (Paris, New York, London). Selected influencers are filmed "shoplifting" from Maje — walking out wearing the clothes. The reveal: They used the Maje Express Pass. The headline writes itself.

2
The Mechanics
Frictionless Execution

Smart hanger NFC/RFID tag. Smart mirror detects items and prompts "Love it? Tap to walk out in it." 1-click settlement via Apple/Google Pay in 1.8 seconds. Green gate validation at exit. Zero queue. Zero waiting.

3
The Engine
Earned Media Architecture

Carrying a shopping bag is old luxury. Walking out wearing a freshly-purchased blazer with zero baggage is the ultimate modern flex. No-Bag Premium — existing clothes couriered home within 2 hrs. Auto-generated TikTok exit video at the gate.

Killed: Safe Alternatives & Why
Styling Appointment Drive
Asks for more of the customer's time when they already suffer from time-friction. Appeals only to planners, not high-traffic impulse browsers.
In-Store Discount Tablets
Cheapens the Maje brand. Discounting in-store to solve a UX problem is a lazy tax on margin.
Standard Mobile POS (Line Busting)
Associates chasing customers with iPads is invasive and awkward. High-fashion buyers want autonomy, not a shadow.
Phase 07

Synthesis

7-Slide Distillation
01
Slide 01 · Challenge
The Store as Gallery. Not Shop.
  • The Symptom: Massive foot traffic, chronically low conversion. The store is an aesthetic gallery, not a point of sale.
  • The Leak: High-intent shoppers try on, admire, and exit — leaving purchase intent to die online later.
  • The Mission: Turn the physical boutique into a high-velocity conversion machine.
02
Slide 02 · Insight
The Hesitation Gap
  • The Showroom Trap: Shoppers love the tactile experience but dread the friction of legacy brick-and-mortar.
  • The Gap: Any friction between "I love this" and "This is mine" allows rational doubt to set in.
  • The Opportunity: Capitalise on peak desire — the exact moment they face the mirror in the garment.
03
Slide 03 · Provocation
Kill the Museum
  • The Shift: Stop treating Maje stores as static showrooms.
  • The Reality: Every second a customer waits to pay is a second they decide not to buy.
  • The Mandate: Transform the boutique into a zero-friction, high-reward acquisition zone.
04
Slide 04 · Territory
Immediate Identity
  • The Territory: Frictionless High-Fashion Acquisition.
  • The Core: Meld the premium, tactile intimacy of the boutique with the rapid, single-tap convenience of digital-native e-commerce.
  • The Goal: Make buying in-store faster, more rewarding, and more personal than ordering online.
05
Slide 05 · Big Idea
Maje: Instantaneous
  • The Concept: An in-store ecosystem that strips away all transactional friction.
  • The Platform: BYPASS — self-checkout instantly from the fitting room.
  • The Reward: Exclusive, immediate styling upgrades unlockable only in-person.
06
Slide 06 · Executions
Three Moves
  • Scan & Go Fitting Rooms: Smart mirrors + QR + instant mobile pay to bypass checkout entirely.
  • The "First Wear" Incentive: Exclusive, in-store-only styling perk for purchases completed within 15 mins of trying on.
  • VIP Fast-Track Stations: Security tag removal and beautiful packaging in under 60 seconds.
07
Slide 07 · Why It Works
The Logic Closes
  • Eliminates Decision Lag: Frictionless checkout at peak desire closes the gap where buyers talk themselves out of a purchase.
  • Leverages Retail Psychology: Instant gratification + immediate exclusive incentives makes buying now feel deeply logical.
  • Optimises Staff: Automating checkout frees associates for high-value personal styling that drives larger basket sizes.
