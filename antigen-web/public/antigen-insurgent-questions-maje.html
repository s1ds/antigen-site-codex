Maje · Physical Retail Conversion
Phase 01 · Naive Enquiry

Naive
Enquiry

What you told me

Maje operates high-footfall boutiques in premium retail locations yet suffers from a persistent and structurally unresolved gap between traffic and conversion. The stores attract browsers in volume but fail to close sales at a rate commensurate with the investment in location, fitout, and brand equity. The problem is framed as a sales execution issue.

What I heard

You are assuming that foot traffic is demand, that the physical store experience faithfully mirrors Maje's aspirational brand positioning, and that the friction preventing conversion lives in the sales conversation rather than in the structural design of the space, the service model, or the measurement system itself. You assume the problem is a skill gap, not a system gap.

The Gap

High traffic at a premium mall location may be logistical spillover, not brand gravity. If the traffic is structurally mismatched to the price point, conversion will always disappoint — regardless of staff skill. There is also a deeper possibility: that the store itself, optimized for a pre-smartphone era of purchase, is now architecturally designed to lose customers who arrive ready to buy.
