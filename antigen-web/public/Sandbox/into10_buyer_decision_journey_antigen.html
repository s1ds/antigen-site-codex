Conversion logic

What INTO 10 must prove

CBO trigger

Revenue leaks in the customer conversation.

The CBO does not buy “learning.” The CBO buys reduced mis-selling, faster launch ramp, sharper objection handling, stronger closures, better service resolution, and less dependence on heroic managers.

CHRO mandate

Capability must become measurable.

The CHRO buys a scalable capability system that moves beyond course completion into role-based readiness, manager coaching, governance, and defensible employee development.

Risk control

The AI claim creates scrutiny.

IT, Legal, Finance, and Procurement will slow the deal unless INTO 10 arrives with security architecture, data handling, SSO, privacy, ROI, and pilot governance assets ready.

Recommended deal motion

Do not sell a platform first. Sell a contained Performance Readiness Pilot. Use one business-critical cohort, one product or process, one set of customer moments, and one measurable business proxy.

1. Capability Leakage AuditName where knowledge decays, where teams improvise, and which customer moments cost money.
2. 10-Day PrototypeConvert one deck, SOP, FAQ, objection bank, or product policy into AI Xpert, practice roleplay, and readiness scoring.
3. 45-Day Paid PilotBaseline users, run simulations, measure readiness lift, manager hours saved, and movement in one business proxy.
4. Role ExpansionExpand into onboarding, launch readiness, sales certification, service recovery, partner training, and manager coaching.
