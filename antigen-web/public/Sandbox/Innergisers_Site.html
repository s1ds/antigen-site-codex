What I do

Five things I train on.

No framework name attached to any of it — just the behaviours that keep showing up when leadership gets hard.

01

Effective communicationSoundwave-certified

How you say something changes whether people hear it. I work on the gap between what leaders mean and what their teams actually receive.

02

Leveraging strengths, working with weaknessesCliftonStrengths

Knowing what you're naturally good at — and what you're not — changes how you lead, delegate and build a team around you.

03

Self-management under pressure

How you regulate yourself when things get tense is the single biggest thing your team is watching and copying.

04

Clarity of decision-making under pressure

Good decisions get harder to make exactly when they matter most. I work on keeping judgement clear when the stakes go up.

05

Collaboration

Building relationships that hold up under disagreement — and finding a real resolution rather than a polite pause.

Communication runs through all five of these in practice — the difficult conversation, the unresolved conflict, the mediation that needs to happen. It isn't a sixth thing. It's the thread that connects the other five.

Case study

New manager transition

Situation
Technically excellent managers struggling with delegation and feedback after their first promotion.
What I did
A strengths assessment, a manager-transition workshop, and follow-up individual coaching.
What changed
Clearer delegation, more direct feedback conversations, and a sharper sense of what each person on the team is actually good at.

Case study

Cross-cultural team collaboration

Situation
A distributed team across India and Singapore reading the same messages very differently.
What I did
Communication profiling and a facilitated conversation that put the misreadings on the table directly.
What changed
Fewer things left unsaid, and a shared language for naming a miscommunication before it turns into a grudge.
