How I work

A light process. Real situations. Practical application.

The process is intentionally simple. Tools such as CliftonStrengths, Soundwave and brain-based coaching support the work where relevant; they are not the proposition.

01

Understand the need

Clarify the business context, people involved and the behaviour that needs to shift.

02

Design the intervention

Choose the right combination of training, coaching, assessment, facilitation and practice.

03

Work on real situations

Use relevant cases, conversations, decisions and team dynamics rather than generic exercises.

04

Practise and apply

Build behaviour through rehearsal, reflection and application in real work.

05

Reinforce

Use follow-up, coaching or team practices where the engagement requires it.

Results & proof

Let the work do the persuading.

The strongest evidence is observable change: how meetings run, how feedback lands, how conflict is handled and how people collaborate afterwards.

Case stories, not claims

Each featured engagement is structured around the situation, what Yamini did, and what changed afterwards. Named client detail is used where permission exists.

I’ve worked inside organisations, led brand and team responsibilities, moved into learning and development, taught in universities, and spent years developing leaders and teams. That mix shapes how I work: commercially grounded, behaviourally specific and practical in the room.
