Enquiry

What you told me

"Multiplier is an EOR and payroll company operating in 160+ countries. You want to scale by targeting large enterprises and rebranding from a service provider into a 'Global Exchange for Work.' This phrase is corporate fluff; I need to know the mechanical reality: are you building a liquidity pool for talent, a new payment rail, or just changing the marketing wrapper on existing legal entity outsourcing?"

What I heard

You assume enterprise buyers find the 'EOR' label too limiting and will be more attracted to a platform or infrastructure play. You are betting that category repositioning will solve the friction of enterprise adoption, and that your current software can handle the compliance nuances of 160+ different legal jurisdictions better than localised competitors.

The Gap

There is a structural contradiction between being an 'infrastructure platform' (a hidden, foundational layer) and an 'exchange' (a dynamic marketplace). You have not defined the specific unit of value being exchanged that justifies the new category. The brief also lacks the technical or operational evidence to prove how your infrastructure differs mechanically from the dozens of other EOR providers making identical global claims.
