Ornua · Regenerative Dairy Pivot
Phase 01 · Naive Enquiry

Naive
Enquiry

What you told me

"Ornua needs to pivot from a product claim ('grass-fed') to a movement ('regenerative dairy') to emulate Patagonia and secure a decade of pricing power." You are using a high-concept marketing narrative to force an operational and cultural transformation across a massive, fragmented cooperative supply chain.

What I heard

You assume the market will commoditize 'grass-fed' and premiumize 'regenerative,' and that repositioning the brand narrative will substitute for the structural, financial, and cultural overhaul required to make that claim real across thousands of independent Irish dairy farmers.

The Gap

You have treated a foundational supply chain overhaul as a positioning exercise, ignoring the structural reality of a cooperative model and the economic trade-offs of lower yields for individual farmers. The Patagonia analogy masks massive structural friction: Patagonia is a vertically integrated private company. Ornua is a co-op owned by thousands of fiercely independent farmers.
