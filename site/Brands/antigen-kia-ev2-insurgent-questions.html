What I Heard

You are prioritizing a numerical leaderboard ranking over specific market share percentages or profitability. You are assuming the German consumer — who historically favors domestic brands like VW and BMW — is ready to switch to a Korean entry-level EV at a volume that exceeds established competitors. You are also using 'vision' as a placeholder for a concrete distribution strategy.

The Gap

Critical Intelligence Gap

The brief offers no mechanical reality: no price point anchored against the VW ID.3, no definition of 'Top 5' (new registrations vs fleet volume vs segment sales), and zero detail on charging infrastructure partnerships or dealer incentive structures needed to fracture local OEM dominance.
