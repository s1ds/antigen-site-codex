The dominant assumption in modern marketing is that more data, optimisation and AI produce better outcomes. This course tests the opposite possibility: that algorithmic saturation is producing strategic and aesthetic homogenisation, and that the marketer’s edge increasingly lies in judgment, taste, ambiguity and cultural foresight.
