Approach

Before you change behaviour, understand what is driving it.

Innergisers combines behavioural science, strengths psychology, communication insight and practical workplace experimentation. The method is simple enough to explain, but rigorous enough to use in real work.

01

NOTICE

See the behaviour. Make the pattern observable through reflection, feedback, observation and assessments.

02

UNDERSTAND

Understand what is driving it through context, strengths, communication preferences and behavioural evidence.

03

INTERRUPT

Create an alternative response using coaching, reframing, scripts, decision rules and practice design.

04

PRACTISE

Use the alternative in real situations through role-play, behavioural experiments and on-the-job application.

05

EMBED

Make the new behaviour easier to repeat through nudges, follow-up coaching, team routines and reflection.
