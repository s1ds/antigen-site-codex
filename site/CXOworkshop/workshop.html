UPGRADE: FROM LINEAR TO COLLISIONAL

TRADITIONAL APPROACH

Process: Linear organizational handover (Brief → Creative Briefing → Media Allocation → Internal Moderation cycles → Delayed Activation).

Result: Highly optimized but utterly standardized assets that survive stakeholder committees but fail to earn category attention.

THE RTC SPRINT PROTOCOL

Process: Collapsed organizational boundaries. All vital components in one physical room (Brand + Creative + Media + PR + Platforms) working collaboratively alongside the core decision-maker for 48 consecutive hours.

Result: Commercially anchored, earned-first creative disruptions developed in real-time and configured for immediate, high-impact cultural integration.

THE MOBILISATION & SPRINT PROTOCOL

T-Minus 3 Weeks Mobilisation: Complete alignment on real business barriers. High-intensity data mining to isolate the consumer and cultural Friction.

Day 1: Collision & Deconstruction: The brief is aggressively interrogated. PR and Media collaborate directly on creative conceptualization from minute one.

Day 2: Rapid Prototyping & Real Judgment: Concepts are fully visualized, copy is committed, and real-channel roadmaps are drafted. The Decision Maker approves live in the room.

The Cannes Jury Verdict: An external jury of verified Cannes Lions practitioners critiques and rates the final output. The benchmark isn't an award—it is the operational standard.
